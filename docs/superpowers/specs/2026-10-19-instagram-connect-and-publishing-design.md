# Instagram connect and scheduled publishing

Date: 2026-10-19
Status: draft, awaiting Gabriel's approval

## Why

Gabriel's brother Augusto (a doctor in Brazil) gets AI avatar videos made in HeyGen. Today the finished videos reach him over WhatsApp and he posts them himself. Gabriel wants Lead Awaker to post them straight to Augusto's Instagram, after Augusto approves each one, so the whole service runs from the platform.

This is also the first piece of a general "social media automation" offer for future Lead Awaker clients. Later projects add Instagram DMs, comments and insights on top of the same connection, so the connection must be built once, for any client account, and never need a reconnect when those features arrive.

This is project 1 of 4, in this order:

1. **Instagram connect + scheduled publishing (this doc)**
2. Augusto's avatar studio (his phone view, script writing in the platform, HeyGen video pull, content calendar). Feeds posts into this scheduler.
3. Comment-to-DM: the live version of the Socials demo (`specs/social-reply-demo/`). Real comments with a trigger word get a private-reply DM, and the conversation runs through the existing AI engine.
4. Insights

## Goals

- Any client account can connect its Instagram professional account (Business or Creator) from the account's Integrations tab, with one tap and an Instagram login.
- The connection requests all five permissions from day one so later projects never need a reconnect.
- The token is stored encrypted and renewed automatically. Gabriel hears about a problem long before the token lapses.
- Agency users can schedule a Reel for a connected account: upload a video, write a caption, pick a date and time, choose whether the client must approve.
- Client users see their own account's scheduled posts and can approve or reject the ones that need approval, from their phone.
- At the scheduled time the server publishes the Reel, labelled as AI-generated by default, and records the result.
- Failures are visible with Meta's reason and can be retried.
- The flow is clean enough to screen-record for Meta's App Review later.

## Non-goals

- DMs, comments, insights (projects 3 and 4). The old Instagram DM setup is left untouched (see "Existing Instagram columns").
- Augusto's avatar studio, HeyGen integration, script writing (project 2).
- Images, carousels, stories, trial reels, collaborators, music, cover images. Reels only.
- Facebook Pages, TikTok, LinkedIn or any other network.
- Submitting the App Review itself (done by hand once this works).
- A content calendar view. The posts list is enough here; the calendar comes with project 2.

## Anti-goals (a failure even if it technically works)

- A post that requires approval goes live without it.
- A post that was held (not approved in time) goes live later on its own at a time nobody picked.
- An access token stored or logged in plaintext.
- One account's videos, posts or connection visible to another account's users.
- A token expiring silently so posts start failing without warning.

## Constraints and facts already established

**Meta app.** Lead Awaker's existing Meta app (the one behind WhatsApp Embedded Signup, already Live, business verified) now also has the "Manage messaging & content on Instagram" use case. Gabriel added these to `.env`, and they are present and well-formed:

- `INSTAGRAM_APP_ID` (the Instagram app ID, different from `VITE_META_APP_ID`)
- `INSTAGRAM_APP_SECRET`

These URLs were entered in the Meta dashboard and must match exactly:

- OAuth redirect: `https://app.leadawaker.com/api/social/oauth/instagram/callback`
- Deauthorize callback: `https://app.leadawaker.com/api/social/instagram/deauthorize`
- Data deletion request: `https://app.leadawaker.com/api/social/instagram/data-deletion`

Augusto has been invited as an Instagram Tester and will accept from his phone. Until App Review, only tester accounts can complete the connect flow.

**API path.** "Instagram API with Instagram Login" on `graph.instagram.com`. No Facebook Page needed. Docs: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform (Meta moved them from `/docs/`).

Scopes requested on every connect:
`instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish, instagram_business_manage_messages, instagram_business_manage_comments, instagram_business_manage_insights`

OAuth facts from the docs:
- Authorize URL: `https://www.instagram.com/oauth/authorize?client_id=…&redirect_uri=…&response_type=code&scope=…&state=…` (optional `enable_fb_login=false`, `force_reauth=true`).
- The returned `code` is single use, valid 1 hour, and may end in `#_` which must be stripped.
- Exchange code for a short-lived token, then exchange that for a long-lived token (60 days).
- Refresh with `GET graph.instagram.com/refresh_access_token?grant_type=ig_refresh_token`, only once the token is at least 24 hours old. An expired token cannot be refreshed; the client must reconnect.

Reels publishing facts:
- `POST /{ig-user-id}/media` with `media_type=REELS`, `video_url`, `caption`, `share_to_feed`, and `is_ai_generated` (field added June 2026; confirm the exact name in the reference at build time).
- Poll `GET /{container-id}?fields=status_code` (`IN_PROGRESS`, `FINISHED`, `ERROR`, `EXPIRED`). Meta suggests once a minute for up to 5 minutes.
- `POST /{ig-user-id}/media_publish` with `creation_id`.
- Containers expire after 24 hours, so create the container at publish time, not earlier.
- No native scheduling. Our server fires the calls.
- Meta downloads `video_url` itself, so it must be a public HTTPS URL.
- Video: MP4 or MOV, moov atom at the front, H.264 or HEVC, AAC up to 48 kHz, 23 to 60 fps, max 1920 px wide, 9:16 recommended, 3 s to 15 min, max 300 MB.
- Caption: max 2,200 characters, 30 hashtags, 20 @ tags.
- Publishing limit: check `GET /{ig-user-id}/content_publishing_limit` (docs say 100 per rolling 24 h).

**Hosting.** The backend runs on the Pi behind a Cloudflare tunnel (`app.leadawaker.com` and `api.leadawaker.com`); the production frontend is on Vercel at `leadawaker.com`. The session cookie spans subdomains via `COOKIE_DOMAIN`, which is what makes the existing calendar OAuth callback on `app.leadawaker.com` work, so follow that pattern exactly. Public video URLs use the Pi backend's domain. Cloudflare limits request bodies to 100 MB on the current plan, so uploads are capped at 100 MB (a 60 s HeyGen 9:16 video is well under that). Cloudflare bot protection must not block Meta fetching `/media/*`; verify on the first real post. `ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` are installed on the Pi.

**Existing Instagram columns.** `Accounts.instagram_access_token` and `Accounts.instagram_user_id` (plaintext, `shared/schema.ts:87-88`) are read by the automations engine's Instagram DM webhook (`/home/gabriel/automations/src/webhooks/instagram_routes.py`, `tools/instagram_service.py`, Graph v19 on `graph.facebook.com`). Only account 1 (Lead Awaker) has values. This project does not read, write or migrate them. Project 3 moves DMs onto the new connection and retires them.

### Who sees and does what

- **Per-account switch** (my call). New column `Accounts.social_enabled` (boolean, default false), toggled by agency users only. The Social integrations group and the Social tab appear for client users only when it's on. Agency users always see the toggle. Reason: until App Review, anyone who isn't an Instagram Tester fails at Meta's login, and existing clients (like account 53) shouldn't see a card that doesn't work for them.
- **Roles** (my call). Agency (Owner/Admin): everything. Client **Manager** on the account: connect, disconnect, approve, reject. Client **Viewer**: sees the tab and posts, can't act. One Manager approval is enough. Approval notifications go to the account's Managers. This needs a new guard (agency, or Manager of that account via `accountIdFor`) instead of `requireAgency` on the connect and approval routes.

### Approval rules (my call)

- Changing the **video or caption** of a post resets `approval_status` to `pending` and notifies the Managers again, whatever it was before.
- Changing only the **time, share-to-feed or AI label** keeps the current approval.
- Rescheduling a **held** post keeps it unapproved; it still needs approval (and re-notifies) before it can publish.
- Agency can switch `requires_approval` off on any post that hasn't started publishing, including a rejected one. This is a deliberate override (for example, the client said "post it" on WhatsApp). The post records who switched it off and when, and the post shows "approval waived by <name>".
- Add `approval_reminder_sent_at` so the 24-hour reminder goes out once.

### Connect flow

Follows the existing OAuth pattern (`server/routes/calendar.ts:63-81`, `server/oauthState.ts`).

- `GET /api/social/oauth/instagram/authorize?accountId=…` (`requireAuth`; the user must be agency, or a user of that account, resolved through `accountIdFor`). Creates a one-time state with `createOAuthState`, then `saveSessionThen` and redirects to the Instagram authorize URL with all five scopes and `enable_fb_login=false`. Same shape as the calendar authorize route.
- `GET /api/social/oauth/instagram/callback` (no auth guard, state checked with `consumeOAuthState`). Strips `#_`, exchanges for short-lived then long-lived token, calls `/me?fields=user_id,username,account_type,profile_picture_url`, stores the granted scopes, upserts `Social_Connections`, redirects back to the account's integrations page with `?instagram=connected` (or `?instagram=error&reason=…`), like the calendar callback. Unlike calendar (agency only, so `FRONTEND_URL` was fine), clients may start from the production site, so the authorize route stores the starting frontend origin in the OAuth state (allowlist: `https://leadawaker.com`, `https://app.leadawaker.com`) and the callback returns there.
- One Instagram account can only be connected to one Lead Awaker account at a time (unique on `provider, external_user_id` among connected rows). A second attempt shows "this Instagram account is already connected to another account".
- A personal (non-professional) account or a user who declines permissions gets a clear message, not a stack trace.
- **Disconnect**: wipes the token and sets `status = disconnected`. Future scheduled posts for that account flip to `failed` with "Instagram disconnected" at their publish time (not immediately, so reconnecting in time saves them).
- `POST /api/social/instagram/deauthorize` and `POST /api/social/instagram/data-deletion`: verify Meta's `signed_request` (HMAC-SHA256 with `INSTAGRAM_APP_SECRET`). Deauthorize marks the matching connection disconnected and wipes the token. Data deletion deletes the connection row and that account's unpublished post files, then returns `{ url, confirmation_code }` pointing at a small public status page.

### Token renewal

A daily job (same shape as `server/reviews/poller.ts`: `running` guard, per-row try/catch, started from `server/routes/index.ts`):

- Refreshes any connected token that expires within 20 days and is at least 24 hours old.
- On failure: `status = needs_attention`, `last_error` set, agency notified through `createAndDispatchNotification`.
- If a token is within 7 days of expiry and still not refreshed, notify the agency daily until fixed.
- Expired tokens: `needs_attention`, card shows "Reconnect".

### Publishing

A poller every 60 seconds (same shape as the reviews poller):

1. **Hold check.** Posts with `status = scheduled`, `scheduled_at <= now`, `requires_approval = true` and `approval_status` not `approved` become `held`. Agency is notified. Held posts never publish on their own: an agency user must pick a new time, which puts them back to `scheduled`.
2. **Start.** Posts with `status = scheduled`, `scheduled_at <= now`, approval satisfied, connection connected: check the publishing limit, create the container with the public video URL, store `ig_container_id`, `status = publishing`.
3. **Finish.** For `publishing` posts, poll the container. `FINISHED`: call `media_publish`, store `ig_media_id`, fetch `permalink`, `status = published`, notify agency and the account's users. `ERROR` or `EXPIRED`, or still `IN_PROGRESS` after 15 minutes: `status = failed` with Meta's message in `last_error`, notify agency.
4. **Retry** (manual, agency only): resets a failed post to `scheduled` at now, clears the container, increments `attempts`.

Rejected posts (`approval_status = rejected`) stay `scheduled` but never start; at their time they become `held` like any unapproved post. The agency sees the client's note.

### Video upload and hosting

- `POST /api/social/posts` (agency only), multipart, streamed to disk under `uploads/social/<accountId>/<uuid>.<ext>`, max 100 MB. Express's JSON limit does not apply; use a streaming parser.
- After upload, run `ffprobe`. Reject anything outside Meta's specs above (format, codecs, duration, fps, width). If only the moov atom is at the end, fix it with `ffmpeg -c copy -movflags +faststart`, no re-encode.
- Public route `GET /media/social/<video_public_id>.<ext>`, no auth, modelled on the `/social-demo-assets` static route in `routes/demoSocial.ts`. Serves only files whose post exists and is not cancelled. Supports range requests. The id is random (not the post id) so files can't be enumerated.
- Files stay after publishing so the posts list can preview them (my call; at a few videos a week this is small, revisit if disk becomes an issue). Deleting or cancelling a post deletes its file.

### Screens

Mobile first, following the existing account workspace styles (`neu-raised`, `cardStyle`) and the en/nl/pt locale files. Augusto uses Portuguese.

**Integrations tab, new "Social" group** (`IntegrationsPanel.tsx`, `ConnectionsOverview.tsx`):
- Instagram card: not connected ("Connect Instagram" button, one line on the Business/Creator requirement), connected (avatar, @username, "Connected", token renews automatically, Disconnect), needs attention (reason, "Reconnect").
- **Client users see and can use this group for their own account.** This is an exception to today's rule that client users only see the "bookings" group, because the client has to log in to Instagram themselves.

**New "Social" tab in the account workspace** (`ACCOUNT_TABS` in `AccountsWorkspace.tsx`):
- Lists posts in sections: needs approval, upcoming, held, failed, published (newest first).
- Each post: video preview (9:16 thumbnail, tap to play), first lines of the caption, scheduled time in the account's timezone (`Accounts.timezone`), status badge, Instagram permalink once published.
- Agency: "New post" sheet (video upload with progress and preview, caption with a 2,200 counter, date and time, share to feed toggle, AI label toggle, requires approval toggle). Agency can edit any post that has not started publishing, reschedule held posts, cancel, retry failed ones.
- Client users of the account: same list, read only. Managers also get **Approve** and **Reject (with note)** on posts waiting for approval. A rejected post can be approved later if the client changes their mind, as long as its time hasn't passed.
- If the account has no Instagram connected, the tab shows the connect card instead.

**Notifications** (existing dispatcher, web push, email, Telegram as configured):
- To the account's users: a post needs your approval (sent when the post is created and again 24 hours before its time if still pending).
- To agency: post held, post failed, post published, client rejected a post, token problems.
