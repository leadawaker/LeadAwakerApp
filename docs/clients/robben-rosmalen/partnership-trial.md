# Partnership Trial Agreement

**Lead Awaker** and **Robben Rosmalen** are starting a trial together. This document puts on paper what we agreed, so we both know what to expect. It is meant to be simple and fair to both sides.

## Who

- **Lead Awaker**, run by Gabriel Fronza, KvK [number], [address] ("Lead Awaker")
- **Robben Rosmalen**, represented by Thijs Robben, KvK [number], Afrikalaan 17a, 's-Hertogenbosch ("Robben Rosmalen")

## What we are trying

Lead Awaker sets up an AI voice assistant that answers Robben Rosmalen's phone calls. The assistant:

- answers questions about the cars in stock (model, year, mileage, price), using the stock that is shown on robbenrosmalen.nl
- answers general questions (opening hours, address, trade-in, private and business lease through POAA)
- takes down the caller's name, number and question when it cannot help
- passes serious buyers on to Thijs (by transferring the call or sending a message)

The same assistant also answers WhatsApp messages and chats with visitors through a chat window on robbenrosmalen.nl, with the same knowledge.

The goal of the trial is simple: find out together whether this saves Thijs time and helps sell more cars.

## How long

The trial runs for **3 months**, from [start date] to [end date].

## What it costs

- **Lead Awaker's work is free during the trial.** Setup, building and adjustments cost nothing.
- **Robben Rosmalen pays only the running costs**, at cost price with no markup: the phone number, call minutes, AI usage and WhatsApp message fees.
- Lead Awaker sends **one invoice per month** with a clear overview of these costs.
- We expect these costs to be around **€40 per month** (excluding VAT). If they look likely to go above **€75 in a month**, Lead Awaker asks first, before spending more.

## What each of us does

**Lead Awaker**
- builds, sets up and maintains the assistant
- keeps the stock information up to date automatically
- reviews calls together with Thijs in the first weeks and improves the assistant based on that
- responds to problems within one working day

**Robben Rosmalen**
- sets up call forwarding to the assistant's number (Lead Awaker helps with this)
- shares the information the assistant needs (prices, rules, when to pass on a call)
- gives honest feedback, good and bad, so we can make it better

## Good to know

- **The assistant can make mistakes.** Prices and availability it mentions are information, not a binding offer. The final deal is always made by Robben Rosmalen.
- **Callers are told** they are speaking with an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded.
- **The data is Robben Rosmalen's.** Call recordings, transcripts and caller details are only used to run the assistant. They are not shared with anyone else and are deleted when the trial ends without a follow-up, unless Robben Rosmalen asks to keep them. We sign a short processing agreement (verwerkersovereenkomst) for this.
- **Showing the results.** Lead Awaker may only use Robben Rosmalen's name or results (for example as a case study) with Thijs's permission.

## Stopping early

Either of us can stop the trial at any time, without a reason, by letting the other know in writing (an email or WhatsApp is fine) **14 days** in advance. Robben Rosmalen then only pays the running costs up to that point. Lead Awaker switches the assistant off and helps undo the call forwarding.

## After the trial

At the end of the 3 months we sit down together and look at the results. Then:

- **If we both want to continue**, we agree on a price together and put it in a new agreement. As one of the first partners, Robben Rosmalen gets a founder partner rate, lower than the regular price.
- **If either of us does not want to continue**, the trial simply ends. Nothing continues automatically, and no costs follow.

## Liability

We both act in good faith. If something goes wrong, we first try to solve it together. Lead Awaker is not liable for indirect damage such as lost sales. Any liability of Lead Awaker is limited to the amounts Robben Rosmalen paid under this agreement.

Dutch law applies to this agreement.
